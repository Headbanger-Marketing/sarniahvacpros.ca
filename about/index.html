About Us

Sarnia's Local HVAC Team, Built on Lambton County Trust

From lake-effect cold snaps in January to the sticky July humidity off Lake Huron, Sarnia HVAC Pros keeps homes across Sarnia, Point Edward, Corunna, and Petrolia comfortable through every season southwestern Ontario throws at us.

Our Story

Rooted in Sarnia, Serving All of Lambton County

We're a family-operated HVAC company based right here on Christina Street North, helping homeowners from Bright's Grove out to Wyoming stay warm, cool, and breathing clean air year-round.

Sarnia isn't just where we work, it's home. We know what Lake Huron and the St. Clair River throw at your furnace in January, how the humidity off the lake pushes an air conditioner into overtime in July, and why a century home near Mitton Village needs a different approach than a postwar bungalow in Coronation Park or a newer build out toward Blackwell. That local knowledge shapes every repair, install, and maintenance visit we do.

Our technicians are licensed, insured, and trained on the full range of residential equipment, from high-efficiency gas furnaces and central air conditioners to ductless mini-splits, cold-climate heat pumps, gas and electric fireplaces, and smart thermostats. Whether you live in a two-storey in Sarnia's east end, a lakeside home in Point Edward, or a rural property near Brigden, we bring the same careful, code-compliant approach to every job.

What started as a small local operation has grown through word-of-mouth across Lambton County, and we plan to keep it that way. Same-day emergency response, honest pricing, and real relationships with the families we serve are the foundation of everything we do.

Our Commitment

Our Commitment to Lambton County Homeowners

Every furnace we install, air conditioner we tune up, and duct system we clean is treated like it's in our own home. We use quality parts, follow Ontario building and TSSA safety codes, and stand behind our workmanship, because in a tight-knit community like Sarnia, reputation is everything and our next customer is usually a neighbour of our last one.

We also believe in straight talk. You'll get a clear explanation of what's wrong, honest options for fixing it, and upfront pricing before any work begins. No inflated diagnoses, no surprise charges on the invoice, and no pressure to replace equipment that still has good years left in it.
